1. THE 60-SECOND OPENING PITCH (Word-for-Word Script)
================================================================================
(When the Professor / Examiner asks: "Explain what you have done in Experiment 8.")

"Good morning / afternoon Ma'am / Sir. Experiment 8 is the capstone synthesis of our entire 
Applied Data Science laboratory curriculum. Our objective was to transition our offline 
trained models into an enterprise-ready, interactive customer support intelligence system 
governed by ethical Responsible AI principles and engineered for live cloud deployment.

We achieved this by building an interactive, multi-tab production dashboard in Streamlit, 
backed by a decoupled inference engine (model_engine.py), persistent SQLAlchemy audit store 
(ticket_store.py), and isolated in a standalone repository (ADS_Main_Production/) deployed 
live on Render PaaS at https://adsproject.onrender.com.

Our system delivers six core enterprise capabilities:

1. Real-Time Customer Triage: Fuses LightGBM multi-class emotion probabilities with VADER 
   sentiment polarity to dynamically categorize customer complaints into CRITICAL, HIGH, 
   MEDIUM, or LOW SLA priority tiers, supporting both interactive text and batch CSV uploads.

2. Dual-Level Explainable AI (XAI): Integrates global n-gram feature importance with token-level 
   waterfall attributions (SHAP approximation) in sub-15ms, showing support agents exactly which 
   words triggered the urgency decision.

3. Automated Privacy Preservation (PII Redaction): Deterministic regex scrubbers redact credit 
   cards, email addresses, phone numbers, and Twitter handles prior to database persistence.

4. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Oversight: Support agents can review AI predictions, adjust urgency 
   levels, record specialist assignments, and append notes with an immutable audit trail.

5. Algorithmic Fairness Auditing: Evaluates Demographic Parity Difference (DPD) and Equalized 
   Odds Difference (EOD) across customer VIP tiers using Fairlearn, reducing disparity by 73.2% 
   (from 0.142 to 0.038) via post-processing threshold optimization.

6. Statistical Data Drift Monitoring: Continuously computes Population Stability Index (PSI) 
   across text length and sentiment distributions against a 5,000-message baseline, with 
   automated retraining alerts."

3. RESPONSIBLE AI GOVERNANCE & FAIRLEARN DISPARITY AUDIT
===============================================================================
Codified in Responsible_AI.md, our system implements five core pillars:

1. Fairness (IEEE 7003 / Fairlearn):
   - Audited Demographic Parity Difference (DPD) across Standard vs. VIP customer tiers.
   - Unmitigated Model: DPD = 0.142, EOD = 0.168 (Violated the 0.05 regulatory threshold).
   - Post-Mitigation (Fairlearn ThresholdOptimizer):
     * DPD reduced to 0.038 (-73.2% reduction, PASS <= 0.050).
     * EOD reduced to 0.044 (-73.8% reduction, PASS <= 0.050).
     * Macro F1-score preserved at 0.806 (99.3% accuracy retention).

2. Privacy & Consent (GDPR / CCPA):
   - Automated regex PII masking for emails, phone numbers, and payment cards.
   - Zero raw query retention policy and GDPR right-to-be-forgotten compliance.

3. Explainability (IEEE 7001 / SHAP):
   - Token-level waterfall feature attributions via leave-one-out perturbation kernel:
     Delta P(emotion) = P(emotion | Text) - P(emotion | Text \ {token})
   - Renders in <15ms directly in the Streamlit UI.

4. Safety & Sandboxing (ISO/IEC 24029):
   - Multi-stage Docker build running under unprivileged user 'appuser'.
   - Fallback heuristic gates for explicit safety keywords (e.g., self-harm, legal threats).

5. Continuous Observability:
   - Population Stability Index (PSI) and Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistical testing.

4. POPULATION STABILITY INDEX (PSI) MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION
===============================================================================
PSI measures the difference between a baseline distribution B and a production batch P 
discretized into K = 10 quantile bins:

   PSI = SUM_{k=1}^{K} (Actual_k - Baseline_k) * ln(Actual_k / Baseline_k)

Governance Interpretation:
- PSI < 0.10          : Stable (Nominal operation, no action required).
- 0.10 <= PSI <= 0.25 : Moderate Drift (Automated operational alert logged).
- PSI > 0.25          : Critical Drift (Automated retraining recommended via DVC).

Why did the live test trigger PSI = 9.665?
"In production, evaluating a small sample of 2 customer tickets against a 5,000-message baseline 
concentrates 100% of the sample into only 1 or 2 bins. All remaining bins have zero counts 
(smoothed with epsilon = 1e-4). This extreme difference causes the logarithmic ratio to spike, 
correctly validating that the threshold alert fires instantly under acute distribution divergence."

5. COMPREHENSIVE VIVA VOCE Q&A (Top 12 Questions & Master Answers)
===============================================================================

Q1: Why is Demographic Parity alone insufficient for auditing algorithmic fairness?
A: Demographic Parity only enforces equal selection rates across groups (e.g., Standard vs. VIP), 
ignoring legitimate underlying base rates. If VIP customers experience more cancellations, forcing 
equal selection rates would misallocate triage resources. Therefore, Equalized Odds must be 
audited concurrently to ensure equal True Positive and False Positive rates across all groups.

Q2: How does the system compute token-level SHAP attributions in real time?
A: In model_engine.py, we implement a localized leave-one-out perturbation kernel. By masking each 
word token one by one and passing the perturbed string through the pre-trained TF-IDF vectorizer 
and LightGBM model, we measure Delta P(emotion) in sub-15ms, avoiding heavy TreeSHAP samplers.

Q3: What role does VADER play alongside the LightGBM emotion classifier?
A: LightGBM predicts categorical emotion (Joy, Anger, Sadness, Fear, Neutral). VADER computes 
a continuous valence polarity compound score (-1.0 to +1.0). Fusing both allows the system to 
differentiate between mild dissatisfaction and severe operational emergencies.

Q4: How does the dynamic SLA calculation work?
A: Urgent safety keywords or Anger/Fear with compound < -0.6 triggers CRITICAL (SLA < 15 min). 
Disappointment/Sadness with compound < -0.3 triggers HIGH (SLA < 1 hr). Neutral inquiries 
trigger MEDIUM (SLA < 4 hr) or LOW (SLA < 24 hr).

Q5: Why is PII masking performed before database persistence?
A: Adhering to the GDPR principle of data minimization (Art. 5), storing raw credit cards or 
emails poses severe security risks. Regex scrubbers sanitize customer text into [EMAIL] and [PHONE] 
before writing to disk, preserving inference capability while protecting privacy.

Q6: How does Render manage cloud container deployments from Git?
A: Render connects to the GitHub repository (ADSproject). Upon detecting a commit to main, Render 
reads render.yaml, builds the multi-stage Dockerfile, binds Streamlit to the dynamic $PORT, and 
periodically probes /_stcore/health to guarantee zero-downtime rolling deploys.

Q7: What is the purpose of the immutable audit trail?
A: To satisfy EU AI Act Level 2 transparency requirements, every ticket creation, status update, 
agent assignment, and resolution note is logged with a UTC timestamp and actor identity in the 
ticket_events table.

Q8: How does the application avoid Streamlit state refresh lag?
A: Upon ticket creation or CSV import, the application triggers st.rerun(), forcing immediate 
re-evaluation of the queue from the database so newly added tickets appear instantly.

Q9: What happens when severe data drift is detected?
A: When PSI exceeds 0.25, the dashboard alerts administrators. In an enterprise MLOps architecture, 
this webhook triggers an automated DVC pipeline to pull newly labeled customer data and retrain 
the LightGBM champion model.

Q10: Why did you use LightGBM over standard Logistic Regression or Random Forest?
A: In our Experiment 3 benchmarks, LightGBM achieved the highest macro F1-score (0.812) with 
sub-45ms inference latency and native gradient-boosted tree efficiency on sparse TF-IDF matrices.
